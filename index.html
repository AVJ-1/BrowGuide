♡ BrowGuide

Try eyebrow shapes on your own photo. Pick a style, then move the guide points to fit your brows.

1. Add your photo

For the easiest fit, use a clear, front-facing photo with your forehead and both eyebrows visible.

Your photo will appear here.
Take a picture or choose one from your device.
Choose a photo to begin. Your photo stays on this device; it is not uploaded by this page.

2. Choose a brow style

3. Adjust the look

Slim ↔ full
Soft ↔ bold

Tip: drag the three guide dots for the selected brow. Move Start to the inner corner, Arch above the highest point, and End toward the outer corner. The labels are shown while adjusting.

4. Make it fit your face

The guide starts in a sample position. After adding your photo, drag the dots to line the brow shape up with your real eyebrows. This version overlays a style guide; it does not automatically recognize facial landmarks.

Save preview exports the photo with the brow overlay as an image.

BrowGuide is a makeup planning aid. Check the shape in a mirror before grooming or removing hair.
