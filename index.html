♡ BrowGuide

Use your live camera and BrowGuide will detect your brow area, recommend a shape, and draw a guide on a captured frame.

1. Use your live camera or choose a photo

Allow camera access when your browser asks. Face the camera in even lighting and keep your forehead and both eyebrows visible. Your camera frames are processed in your browser.

Tap “Start live camera” or choose a photo to begin.
Start the live camera and capture a frame, or choose a photo. Then tap “Detect my brows.”

2. Your recommendation

Your recommended brow shape will appear here after detection.

3. Personalize the diagram

The diagram uses detected facial landmarks to estimate brow placement. It is a cosmetic guide, not a professional measurement.

For best results, check the guide in a mirror before grooming. Face shape alone cannot determine personal style or preference.
