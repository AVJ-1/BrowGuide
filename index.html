Find Your Brow Placement

Learn where to start, where to create your arch, and where your brow can end.

Choose a marker below to explore each step. Every face is different — use these as flexible guidelines, not strict rules.

Illustration is a learning guide, not a measurement tool. You can use a clean makeup brush or pencil as a straight edge.

Where to Place Your Brows

1

Start · Inner Corner

Hold a makeup pencil vertically beside the outside of your nostril. Where it meets your brow area is a useful starting guide.

2

Arch · Highest Point

Angle the pencil from the side of your nose through the outer edge of your iris while looking straight ahead. This can help find a natural high point.

3

End · Outer Corner

Angle the pencil from the side of your nose toward the outer corner of your eye. The line gives you a guide for where the tail may finish.

✧ Quick Tips

  • Mark lightly with a brow pencil before filling in.
  • Compare both brows, but remember faces are naturally asymmetrical.
  • Use small, hair-like strokes for a softer look.
  • Step back and check your brows in natural light.
